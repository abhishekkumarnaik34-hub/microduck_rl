## 🧪 What Is Reinforcement Learning?

Reinforcement learning sounds complicated, but here's a simple way to think about it:

Imagine teaching a puppy to sit. You don't explain physics or commands - you reward the puppy when it does the right thing and gently guide it when it's wrong. Over time, the puppy connects actions with rewards.

That's exactly what microduck_rl does! The virtual duck tries random movements, and when it does something that leads toward success (like taking a step forward without falling), it gets a reward. When it fails, it gets a small penalty. Through thousands of attempts, the duck's "brain" figures out which actions lead to rewards and starts doing them more and more.

The result is a duck that genuinely learns - and you get to watch the whole amazing process! 🎉
